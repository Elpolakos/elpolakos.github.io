🎯 What Is This?

lab-instrument-mcps is a collection of free, open-source tools that connect your AI assistants to your laboratory instruments. Think of it as a universal translator that lets ChatGPT or other AI agents understand and control scientific equipment like temperature controllers, sensors, or measurement devices.

The software is designed with safety first - it includes multiple protection layers so AI can help in the lab without causing accidents or damage. Every action is recorded in an audit trail, so you always know what happened and when.

You don't need any programming experience to use it. Simply download, run, and connect your AI assistant to start automating repetitive lab tasks.

✨ Key Features

🛡️ Safety Limits

Every instrument has hard safety boundaries that AI cannot cross. No more worries about extreme temperatures, pressures, or speeds.

🔍 Read-Only Mode

Just want your AI to observe and analyze data? Enable read-only mode and it can monitor everything without touching any controls.

📝 Audit Trail

Every single action is logged - who did what, when, and why. Perfect for compliance and troubleshooting.

🎮 Built-in Simulator

Every instrument comes with a virtual simulator. Practice how AI works with your equipment before going live.

🧪 Broad Instrument Support

Works with scientific instruments of all types - from simple sensors to complex analytical machines.

🚀 Self-Driving Lab Ready

Built for the future of lab automation. Let AI plan, run, and analyze your experiments 24/7.
